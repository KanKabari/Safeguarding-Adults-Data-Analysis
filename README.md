# Safeguarding Adults Data Analysis

### 🧠 Overview

This project explores insights from the *Safeguarding Adults Collection* dataset to understand key patterns in reported concerns, enquiries, and outcomes across different demographic groups. The goal is to identify trends that can help local authorities improve safeguarding decisions and ensure adults at risk receive the right support.

### 🎯 Objectives

-   Analyse who is most affected by safeguarding concerns (age, gender, ethnicity, support needs).
-   Understand what kinds of risks are most common and where they occur.
-   Assess how risks are managed and whether outcomes align with individuals’ wishes.
-   Recommend actionable steps to improve safeguarding practices and data recording.

### 📊 Key Insights

-   **Demographics:** Over half of all concerns involve older adults (65–95+), especially women.
-   **Progression:** Only about **5.3%** of concerns progress to formal enquiries.
-   **Risk Patterns:** Neglect and physical abuse are the most reported risks, often involving known individuals or service providers.
-   **Locations:** Most risks occur in people’s own homes or care facilities.
-   **Mental Capacity:** Older adults (especially 85–94) are more likely to lack mental capacity or have “not known” status recorded.
-   **Engagement:** Most individuals were involved in decisions, but recording of desired outcomes can be improved.

### 🧩 Recommendations

-   Train staff to better identify and record safeguarding concerns, especially for older women.
-   Investigate why so few concerns progress to enquiries.
-   Improve documentation around capacity assessments and outcome discussions.
-   Reduce “Don’t know” responses by providing advocacy and communication support.

### 🛠️ Tools Used

-   **Excel** – Data cleaning and preparation
-   **PowerPoint** – Presentation of key findings
-   **R Language** – For data transformation, exploration, and visualization (using **ggplot2** package)
